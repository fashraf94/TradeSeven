## Ruling of record — `decide.js` (REVERSED, Aug 6, 2026)

The prior ruling ("DERIVED classification ACCEPTED; no PR-4 splice required") is **REVERSED by the Sol adjudication**: the DERIVED classification **stands for dark operation only**. **The PR-4 fenced splice is now REQUIRED** — epoch/generation validation before the `activeRules` write, plus a **generation stamp** on the written projection, with readers rejecting stale-generation projections.

**Recorded reason — Sol's cross-generation counterexample:** the transitive-fence argument freezes the projection's inputs *within* an epoch; it says nothing across an **activation boundary**. A decide.js window in flight during activation can read the authority stores pre-activation (generation N−1), then commit its re-derived `activeRules` projection *after* generation N activates — persisting an old-identity projection past the watermark with nothing marking it stale, indistinguishable to readers from a current one. Input-purity does not survive a generation flip mid-window; only write-time validation plus a reader-checked stamp does.

- **Closing item (PR 4):** the sanctioned fenced splice (§7-gated fence contact, founder-approved by this ruling) — validate epoch + generation before the write, stamp `projectionGeneration` on the written value; the deploy path and assemblers reject a stamp ≠ current generation.
- **Acceptance test:** a projection write carrying generation N−1 attempted after activation of N is rejected at the write; a stale-stamped projection already persisted is rejected by the reader path (both directions covered).
