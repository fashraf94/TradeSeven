## D. Ordering invariant + provisional dial bands (with precise semantics)

**Base ordering invariant** (design §5.2, item 6): `Speculator > Trend Follower > … > Capital Preserver`. Holds on the tuned values at every gate preset: `degen ≫ mc > analyst≈diversifier≈contrarian > guardian`.

**Provisional dial bands** — `[ Measured | Standard | Aggressive ]`, multipliers **`0.7 / 1.0 / 1.3`** applied to the *resolved* knob config. **Verified across the full archetype × dial cross-product on TOTAL tempo** (forced + haiku executed — the shared swap-window couples the two paths, so per-path numbers mislead): per-band ordering holds and **Capital Preserver @ Aggressive stays slower than Speculator @ Measured** on trend/chop/stress.

**Band application semantics (rider 3 — the unambiguous definition WS2 inherits).** Multiplier `mult` ∈ {0.7, 1.0, 1.3}, applied **direction-aware** so every band change points the same way (higher band → more tempo):

| Field | Type | Transform | Clamp |
|---|---|---|---|
| `swapWindow.capPerWindow` | capacity ("more") | `round(cap × mult)` | ≥ 1 |
| `forcedRotation.ticksThreshold` | resistance (threshold) | `round(ticks ÷ mult)` | ≥ 1 |
| `hurdleFloor.byReason.haiku_decision.atrMultiplier` | resistance (floor) | `round2(floor ÷ mult)` | — |
| `hurdleFloor.byReason.stagnation.atrMultiplier` | resistance (floor) | `round2(floor ÷ mult)` | — |
| `hurdleFloor.default.atrMultiplier` | resistance (floor) | `round2(floor ÷ mult)` | — |

**Left untouched (structural + safety — preserved verbatim at every band):** `forcedRotation.enabled` (guardian stays disabled), `pctThreshold`, `winnerThreshold`, `maxTickAgeMinutes`, `swapWindow.windowMinutes`, `swapWindow.countEmergencies`, `hurdleFloor.enabled`, `requireBenchPositive`, and the `EMERGENCY_BYPASS_REASONS` set. The dial modulates tempo thresholds/magnitudes **only** — never the rung-1 emergency-bypass set or circuit-breaker safety semantics (design §5.3). These band multipliers are **provisional**; WS2 authors the final bands from this report.

**F2 — Wake-starvation = FAILED-STRUCTURAL (8C).** The pre-Haiku wake trigger (`changePct/benchATR`, bench-only) and the hurdle floor (`(bench−active)/atr`, relative) diverge, so the haiku path is wake-starved ~51% in chop — a swap the hurdle would allow that the wake never surfaces. **No knob value closes this** (it is the formula divergence, not the thresholds). **Scoped fix:** 8C unification inside the fenced `evaluateTriggers` — a fence-aware queue item with its **own** wake-threshold re-calibration, reusing this harness. **Not a calibration blocker; not tuned toward.**

**F3 — Rulings recorded (B3 approval).**
- **degen `hurdleFloor.stagnation` 0.6 → 0.3 is a deliberate rationale evolution, not a tuning artifact.** It **supersedes the V1.4 "high mechanical-rotation bar" rationale on archetype-identity grounds** — the Speculator refuses boring, and the June 2026 archetype definitions postdate the V1.4 rationale. The **Shape-B principle survives directionally** (stagnation floor 0.3 > haiku floor 0.2 — a stagnation rotation still faces a stricter bar than a discretionary one). **Knob C's cap remains the churn bound** (degen cap 12/60min is the hard ceiling on churn; lowering the floor changes rotation *readiness*, not the *ceiling*).

## Real-Data Cross-Check (framing, promotion gate, and post-landing confirmation)

**Framing (recorded per B4 acceptance addendum).** The real `agentBattles` were generated **under the shipped illustrative values and largely pre-A1 ATR** — so the cross-check **cannot confirm the proposed deltas** (the deltas didn't exist when those battles ran). It serves three purposes only:
- **(a) ordering-direction validation** — do the real trades[] show `degen > mc > … > guardian` in tempo *at all* (the direction the tuning strengthens)?
- **(b) the pre-change baseline** — the "before" real numbers, for an apples-to-apples post-landing comparison.
- **(c) emergency + taxonomy checks** — emergency-bypass rare/reason-attributed, and the unknown-reason share (default-deny inflation) quantified.

**Promotion definition (revised):** `provisional → calibrated` = **(harness verification, done) + (real ordering-direction check, now)** — with a **scheduled post-landing re-run of `aggregate-real-battles.js`** on battles generated *under the landed values* as the **true confirmation** of the deltas.

**Run now (baseline read; sandbox has no creds → Flash runs it):**
1. `node scripts/calibration/export-agent-battles.js` (read-only) → `export.json`.
2. `node scripts/calibration/aggregate-real-battles.js --input export.json` → per-archetype tempo direction, 8B stagnation share, emergency-bypass (reason-attributed), unknown-reason share + censored flags.
3. **Accept** if: real tempo ordering-direction holds (degen highest, guardian lowest); emergency-bypass is rare/attributed; the unknown-reason share is small enough that default-deny inflation doesn't carry the direction (if material → treat as provisional until the taxonomy is reconciled). This is a **direction + baseline** acceptance, not a delta confirmation.

**Post-landing (the true confirmation — schedule it):** after the Tier-2 bundle lands the tuned values, re-run `aggregate-real-battles.js` on the *new* battles and confirm the 8A multiples / 8B bands / degen↔mc separation moved as predicted vs the pre-change baseline. Only then are the deltas confirmed on real data.
