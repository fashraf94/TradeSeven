## The rulings

1. **Timing.** A corrected position's quote lower bound is its fill quote's provider time (`fillQuote.quoteAsOf`). A strictly later quote is valid; a quote at the same provider time is valid only at exactly the fill price; same time with a different price is unavailable. An exit needs a quote strictly later than the fill quote. `enteredAt` stays recorded and remains the upper bound on fill evidence. Branch A: a tick quote that predates the fill values the position at its own receipt fill (same time, same price). The `stockEntryCommit` marker is deleted, so K2-6 disappears.
2. **Late-start cutoff.** Corrected creation is refused from 45 minutes before the calendar close (15:15 ET normally, 12:15 ET on early-close days), measured at the creation instant against `getSessionForDate(...).closeMs`, in `prepareStockCreation` before any measurement GET. Retriable, with a clear player message. Only an allowlisted creation can reach it. *Superseded by BR-2 (Oct 25, 2026): the refusal is removed; from the same 45 minutes the battle plays the next session.*
3. **Missing reward evidence.** The four corrected early returns in `processAgentBattle` continue the tick. The calibrated risk pass, forced exits and deployed guardrails (including the R11 pass) run; reward-dependent stages see an unavailable context; `finalizeCronState` and the normal final write happen on every corrected exit; a null score is never written or shown as 0. The M1 gate is unchanged.
4. **Expiry.** An expired corrected battle settles on its latest stored frame if that frame is valid; otherwise it ends as result unavailable (no last-valid-frame record). The terminal write follows Phase 0 §E3 and does not depend on `readCorrectedInputs`; it also covers route `unavailable`. Both pattern-logger callers check the disposition.
5. **Pilot allowlist.** Keyed on `ownerId` (Ruling 14); details in Ruling 6.
6. **The switch moves to the control document.** `STOCK_MEASURED_THRESHOLDS_ADMISSION_ENABLED = true` ships with its pins reconciled in the same commit. `stockThresholdControl/admission` (schema 2, carrying `audience`) is the only on-switch. Missing, disabled, unparseable or old-schema control means legacy for everyone, with no measurement reads; this reverses the v1 runbook's "never legacy" for unselected creations. Only `audience: { mode: 'allowlist', ownerIds: [...] }` (non-empty) selects, and only for listed owners; any other mode, including a future `'all'`, is closed and legacy. *(BR-4, Oct 25, 2026, opens exactly `{ mode: 'all' }` as a second selecting audience.)* A selected creation that sees the control change before commit is refused. Approved fence contact: `agentBattleService.js` keys on per-battle selection. Tournament creation stays byte-identical.
7. **Off-hours creation is allowed** (pre-open, after close, weekends). The fill is the latest valid real-time quote at creation, with the strict `close` field, a provider timestamp and the existing activation validation. The opening jump counts as post-fill movement. The 16:00–16:15 ET publication-window refusal stays. Source-session closing-quote rule, from the final Phase 0 §C3: accepted when its provider stamp is at or after the source session's calendar close (the closing bar is stamped exactly at the close); an earlier stamp (the 15:59:00 minute bar still served just after 16:15) is refused, retriably; no tolerance.
8. **Pilot crypto.** An incoming crypto fill with `fallback: true` is refused; the outgoing crypto entry gets the positive check; normal crypto swaps continue.
9. **Strict close.** Corrected stock valuation accepts only a real `close`; the `previousClose` stand-in is unavailable for corrected stocks (an additive field from the shared path; legacy selection unchanged).
10. **Agent instructions.** Approved fence contact in `agentEvalPromptAssembly.js`: corrected games get reward text naming the frozen measured percentage as the divisor, with matching worked examples; legacy prompts and goldens stay byte-identical; the split renderer joins `PROMPT_CONTRIBUTING_MODULES` in the same commit.
11. **Result-unavailable side effects.** Retire the vision. Write no settlement record, stats, mastery stamp, reflection, review queue or pattern log.
12. **Completion timing** (20:00 ET expiry, completion at the next weekday's first run) is accepted as-is.
13. **v2 policy content**, as in the identity table above.
14. **Allowlist key is `ownerId`.** FT_QA owns two agents named Cipher; the account is the unit.
15. **The stock modal on corrected positions** uses the normal BaggerBomb tab layout from the committed context only, draws all six targets on the chart from the fill with HIT markers only from crossed tiers, puts references in a collapsed Details row, states "Measured reward unavailable" clearly and fills Why's proximity text. For every battle, the placeholder "Historical (30 days)" block is removed and no rating renders without a real `ratingText`.

## Switch-on preconditions (build findings, now ruled)

The v2 build found two problems that blocked switch-on. Flash ruled on both in the blockers follow-up (Fable's prompt of Oct 15, 2026, received by the follow-up session at about 21:50 America/Chicago, `2026-10-16T02:50Z`), together with a display ruling (P3). None of the three is written into the hashed policy: the v2 policy hash, the publication-rule hash, the approved reference and `approvedAt` are all unchanged. P2 does narrow what the hashed `offHoursCreation.allowed` entry `after_close_once_publication_window_opens` permits, for pilot creations only; this record, not the policy file, carries that narrowing. No receipt existed when they landed, so nothing was migrated. Switching the pilot on remains a separate, authorized step.

**1. Security identity (ruling P1, the v2 build report's §6a Option A).** The collector (`collectStockObservation`, `api/_utils/stockThresholdMeasurementProvider.js`) keeps every other identity check exactly as before: `Code`, `IsDelisted === false`, NYSE/NASDAQ, "Common Stock", US/USA. Then:
- When `General.ISIN` is a non-empty string, `securityId` is `EODHD:<SYM>.US:<ISIN>`, as before.
- Otherwise `General.OpenFigi` is accepted only when it is a well-formed FIGI: 12 characters, the OpenFIGI shape (two consonants, `G`, eight consonants or digits, a digit), no reserved prefix (BS, BM, GG, GB, GH, KY, VG) and a valid check digit. `securityId` is then `EODHD:<SYM>.US:FIGI:<OpenFigi>`.
- If neither is present, the stock is refused `security_identity_unqualified`, as before.
- CIK and LEI are never used. The provider's `OpenFigi` is the US composite FIGI, one per security, so the two share classes of one company differ (BRK-A `BBG000DWDMY4`, BRK-B `BBG000DWH8Q0`), while a CIK is shared by both.
- Receipts carry the id, and every replay compares it (`readCorrectedInputs`), whichever form it takes.
- **Universe record** (real collector path, GET only, concurrency 1, no Firestore; Oct 15, 23:09 ET): 228 of 239 qualify (8 with the ISIN form, 220 with the FIGI form). The other 11 are the catalog exclusions (AVB, CTRA, EA, EQR, HES, IPG, K, MMC, PARA, WBA, WBD). UNP, HUM and ACN qualify.

**2. The evening window (ruling P2, the v2 build report's §6b option (a)).** *Superseded by BR-2 (Oct 25, 2026): the `evening_window` refusal and its player line are removed, and an after-close creation plays the next session. The text below is the Oct 15 record.*
- **The problem.** On a regular trading day, a full-day battle holding crypto that is created after the close expires at 20:00 ET the same evening (the fenced `computeFullDayExpiry`, Night Game close). No evaluation runs after 16:00 ET, so it would never get a scored check.
- **The rule.** For selected (allowlisted) creations only, `prepareStockCreation` now refuses retriably with `evening_window` when the creation is outside a trading session (after the current session's close, or on a weekend or holiday) and the battle's own `expiresAt` falls before the next trading session's `openMs`.
  - It runs after selection and before any measurement GET.
  - It is derived from the calendar (`getSessionForDate`) and the battle's `expiresAt` (`eveningWindowRefusal`, `src/utils/stockAdmissionWindow.js`), never from a clock literal.
  - It begins at the close, where Ruling 2's 45-minute late start ends, so the two leave no gap.
- **This narrows Ruling 7 for the pilot only**, pending the separate diagnosis of the legacy evening 0–0 draws. Non-selected owners, legacy creation and tournaments are unchanged. The fence is not touched.
- **When a crypto roster can start, on a regular trading day.**
  - Refused from 15:15 to 20:00 ET (14:15 to 19:00 CT).
  - Allowed from 20:00 ET (19:00 CT) until the next session's late start (15:15 ET, or 12:15 ET when that session closes early), including pre-open, weekends and holidays.
  - A Friday creation from 15:15 ET is refused until 20:00 ET; Saturday is allowed.
- **Early-close days.** A crypto roster's expiry is the 13:00 ET close itself; there is no Night Game. So:
  - the late start refuses 12:15 to 13:00 ET (11:15 to 12:00 CT);
  - the collector's publication window refuses 13:00 to 13:15 ET;
  - from 13:15 ET the battle plays the next session.
- **Stock-only rosters** play the next session after the close and are not refused by this rule. In practice none reaches the pilot: a tiered decision without its support crypto is refused (`decide.js` `validatePortfolio`), so every pilot roster carries crypto.
- **The player line**, for both `late_start_cutoff` and `evening_window` (`src/services/agentDeploy.js`), names the retry time from the same module (`stockAdmissionRetryAt`): "Measured battles can't start this late in the day. Try again after 8:00 PM ET." On an early-close day it says 1:15 PM ET. The server's 503 (fenced `decide.js`) carries only the reason, so the browser computes the time from its own clock when the refusal arrives. That time assumes the roster carries crypto (above); a browser clock set to another day falls back to the generic line or names that day's time.

**3. Why proximity (ruling P3, review B3-7).** On measured rows, Why's proximity line follows the direction, as legacy does:
- below the fill it shows the committed `penaltyProximity` (for example "📉 0.8% to Bust"; "🔥 MAX" once every penalty tier is crossed);
- at or above the fill it shows `proximity`, as before.

It reads the committed context only (§9 display agreement). Legacy rows are unchanged.

## Broad release (BR-1 to BR-4), Oct 25, 2026

Flash's rulings, as carried by Fable's broad-release prompt (the pilot ran for Flash and FT_QA from Oct 16; Flash found the thresholds much better in his own battles and wants them for every account):

- **BR-1 — Everyone.** Every new `baggerbomb_agent` full-day battle uses the measured model. Existing battles keep their model. Tournament and League battles and the player modes stay on their current contracts, byte-identical. The control document stays the switch, so rollback remains one command.
- **BR-2 — Late-day starts play the next trading day. No evening block.** On a trading day, a tiered full-day battle created at or after 45 minutes before the calendar close (15:15 ET; 12:15 ET on early-close days) targets the next trading session's close, with the same crypto Night Game close rule that session would normally get. Creations at any other time are unchanged. This applies to legacy creations too, which ends the legacy evening 0–0 draws, and to measured creations. The `late_start_cutoff` and `evening_window` refusals and their player lines are removed. The collector's publication wait stays: creation from the close until close + 15 minutes is refused retriably, with the line "Measured battles open again at 4:15 PM ET" (1:15 PM on early-close days). Tournament creation is unchanged. Approved fence contact: `computeFullDayExpiry` / `createAgentBattle` in `api/_utils/agentBattleService.js`, for this rule only, derived from the calendar with no dependence on the process time zone; `expiresAt`, `tradingDays` and `localClose` agree. **BR-2 supersedes Ruling 2 (Oct 15) and the pilot's ruling P2.**
- **BR-3 — Retire the 11 dead tickers** (AVB, CTRA, EA, EQR, HES, IPG, K, MMC, PARA, WBA, WBD) from the pickable universe (`STOCK_UNIVERSE`, `api/_utils/rankingConfig.js`). No successors. Existing documents and battles that hold them are untouched.
- **BR-4 — The switch.** The registry accepts a new audience `{ mode: 'all' }` alongside `allowlist`; the control script gains `enable --all`. Missing, disabled, unparseable or old-schema control still means legacy for everyone. A mid-flight change is still refused at commit. If any hashed v2 policy field must change, it is versioned the existing way; otherwise the v2 model and hashes stay.

**How they were built** (the build report has the detail and citations):

- **BR-2.** The fenced `createAgentBattle` takes a tiered battle's full day from `tieredFullDayTarget` (`src/utils/stockAdmissionWindow.js`): the calendar of record at the creation instant, through Intl only. Before the late-day start on a trading day it is that session; from it, and on a weekend or holiday, it is the next session. The expiry is that session's close, or 20:00 ET for a crypto roster on a regular day. Outside the late-day window this equals the old rule at every quarter hour through Dec 31, 2027 (swept). Tournaments still call the unchanged `computeFullDayExpiry`. A creation the calendar cannot place is refused retriably, `calendar_missing`. That happens from Fri Dec 31, 2027, 15:15 ET, because the next session lies in 2028, outside the tables, and it now applies to legacy tiered creation too, until the 2028 tables land in `src/utils/marketCalendar.js`.
- **BR-3.** The 11 leave `STOCK_UNIVERSE` and `ALL_TICKERS` (239 → 228), so no cron fetches or ranks them and the next `stockRankings` rebuild drops them from the ranked pool the CPU and the agent draw from. The agent's one other source, an equipped watchlist (decide.js folds equipped tickers in even when off the ranked list), can still carry a ticker the list already holds; no committed production watchlist held one of the 11 on Oct 25, the UI and the dialogue validator refuse new adds, the watchlist save endpoints check shape only, and a measured creation refuses all 11 (`catalog_unavailable`). They keep their last sector in the label maps (`RETIRED_STOCKS` → `TICKER_TO_SECTOR` / `TYPE` / `INDUSTRY`), so existing battles, Film Room tapes, watchlists and Wire documents read as before. The ticker validator and the Forge add search refuse new picks of them. `WIRE_GENERATION_VERSION` moves 23 → 24 for the `ALL_TICKERS` value lock; the Wire universe (`TICKER_TO_SECTOR`) and the validator version do not move.
- **BR-4.** Only exactly `{ mode: 'all' }` opens (an `'all'` carrying ids or any other key stays closed). It selects every owner's tiered full-day creation and still needs an owner id; tournaments never read the control. `enable --all` and `--owner` are exclusive; `disable` keeps the audience.
- **Hashed policy: no field changes, so the v2 model and hashes stay.** The hashed `rollout` entries read `activation: control_document_allowlist_pilot_keyed_on_ownerId`, `audience: allowlist_mode_with_non_empty_owner_ids_only_any_other_mode_closed` and `broadRelease: later_code_change_and_owner_decision`. BR-4 is that later code change and owner decision, and nothing in the code reads `rollout`. So BR-4 supersedes the `rollout.activation` and `rollout.audience` wording for admission, and this record (not the policy file) carries it, as P2's narrowing of `offHoursCreation` was carried here before. `offHoursCreation` (pre-open, after close once the publication window opens, weekends and holidays; the publication window refused) is unchanged by BR-2. Re-versioning would have stranded the live pilot receipts unless the registry kept v2 beside a v3. `currentCatalogExceptions` stays accurate. The policy file's unhashed `unhashedCodeConstants` now names BR-2's `LATE_DAY_START_MINUTES` (the same 45).

## Build readings of the rulings (recorded, not new rulings)

- **Unreadable control (Ruling 6).** A control document that cannot be read is treated like a missing one: legacy creation, no measurement read, and a server warning that names no owner. Refusing instead would 503 every tiered creation on a Firestore blip, which Ruling 6 forbids for non-listed owners. An allowlisted owner can therefore get a legacy battle during an outage; the pilot verify script reports such a battle as `route legacy`.
- **A late-day battle also plays the rest of its creation day (BR-2).** The battle targets the next session's close, but nothing holds its evaluation back until that session: a battle created from 15:15 to 15:59 ET is evaluated by that day's 15:30 and 15:45 ticks too, and the daily cron's evening record for it (`agent-daily-scores`, 01:45 UTC: 21:45 ET in daylight time, 20:45 ET in standard time) is written that evening under `day1`. On a measured battle that record is a frame checkpoint only (the score is lifetime from the fill, and Ruling 4 settles on the latest frame); on a legacy battle it banks the creation day's points as day 1. The Film Room writes a tape only for dates in `tradingDays`, so those creation-day ticks and any swaps they make score but appear on no tape. Only creations from 15:15 to 15:44 ET get such a tick (the 16:00 run is outside market hours). **On the legacy route only**, the night-D reset also clears the entry price of any position swapped in on D (`agent-daily-scores.js` clears `swapPrice` for day-1 swaps), so on D+1 that position is scored from the creation-time starting price, or from 0 when it came off the hot bench with none (`agent-evaluate.js` entry-price fallback). The measured route skips that reset (`checkpointCorrectedDaily` records a frame only), so under `enable --all` this applies only to legacy creations: before the switch, or after a rollback. This build adds no cron or tape change (outside the approved contact); the build report asks Flash whether the rest-of-today ticks are wanted.
- **The 11 can still reach a pool until the rankings rebuild (BR-3).** The CPU and the agent draw from `indexIntelligence/stockRankings`, which keeps its old list until `compute-index-intelligence` next runs on a weekday after the deploy (10:30 / 11:30 UTC, then hourly 14:00–20:00 UTC). Until then a measured creation that draws one is refused retriably by the collector (`catalog_unavailable`). `enable --all --apply` therefore reads the document and refuses while any of the 11 remains (`scripts/stock-threshold-pilot-control.mjs`).
- **Equipped tickers off the ranked list refuse a measured creation (BR-1).** decide.js keeps an equipped watchlist's tickers in the agent's pool even when they are not in `stockRankings` (an ETF such as KRE; 5 agents' equipped watchlists held KRE on Oct 25). When the agent places one, a measured creation is refused retriably (the collector's identity check or the commit's ranked-membership check), after the deploy's model calls. Under the pilot only the two allowlisted owners could meet it; under `all` everyone can. Recorded as a question for Flash before `enable --all`; no BR covers it.
- **Past the calendar (BR-2).** From Fri Dec 31, 2027, 15:15 ET every tiered creation, legacy included, is refused `calendar_missing` until the 2028 tables land; a player sees the generic retry line. Tests that create a tiered battle on the real clock go red from that instant too (14 rows in 3 files, per the build review).
- **No tick quote (Ruling 1).** Branch A stands in only for a dated, positive tick quote that predates the fill, as ruled. A position whose tick quote is absent stays unavailable for that frame; if that is the final frame (a 15:45 ET swap whose quote fetch failed), Ruling 4 ends the battle as result unavailable.
