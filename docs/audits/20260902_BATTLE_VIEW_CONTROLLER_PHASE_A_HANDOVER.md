**The five steps from the first smoke, again** (they must still hold under the new layout):

1. **The header** shows `Checked 12:47 PM · next ~1:02 PM` beneath the tug-of-war bar. Wait through one check: the rows wash top to bottom and the line ticks once (≤700 ms); nothing moves in between. Off-hours: `Market closed · last check 3:45 PM · next Tue 9:30 AM ET` (with no check yet that day: `Market closed · next check Tue 9:30 AM ET`); pre-open: `First check at 9:30 AM ET`; late: `Last check 12:47 PM · next was due ~1:02 PM`.
2. **Tap your SLB row**: the panel opens beneath the row — `This piece today` (if it traded), `At the 12:47 PM check` + the state, the agent's own words with `SLB` emphasised, the facts line (the same `% to` number the row shows, `Entry $…`, `Held since …`), no lock line. The CPU side never opens. Tap the score header: the book panel (the decision → the door; **This turn is no longer inside it** — it has one home, above the board).
3. **A tick the guardrail held** reads `Argued for a swap · held by a guardrail` / `The agent's own words · the system held it`. A tick whose swap **failed to execute** reads `Argued for a swap · it did not go through` / `The agent's own words · the position stayed as it was` (fixture-guaranteed: `selectWhyState.test.js`, `WhyPanel.render.test.jsx`). A tick whose model call **timed out** reads `No decision recorded at this check · the evaluation timed out`; any other outage (budget-skipped, a truncated response, an HTTP status) reads the plain `No decision recorded at this check` (§4 item 20).

**The A4 steps:**

6. **Desktop (≥ 768 px):** no tab bar. The board is left (~60 %), the chat is right (~40 %) with its composer at the bottom, both under the score header; the board column scrolls on its own, the tier headers stick; the book panel opens between the header and the Film Room banner; the banner keeps its slot above the board. 7. **Mobile:** header, *This turn*, the board as the page; the chat is a sheet at the bottom, at **peek**: the turn line and the composer only. Tap the handle → **half** (the conversation; the handle now reads `Show more of the chat` to a screen reader); tap again → **full** (the header's Back bar stays visible above it); tap again → **peek**. 8. **The door on both:** desktop — `Ask a follow-up · 1 message` focuses the composer with `About SLB — ` and nothing else changes. Mobile — the sheet opens to half (or stays full), then the composer is focused with the prefill; collapse the sheet and focus returns to the door. Type a few words first, then use the door: your words stay.
9. **The unread dot (mobile):** with the sheet at peek, wait for a feed entry (a check): a dot appears on the handle (`· new activity` in its name); open the sheet — it clears. On desktop there is no dot (the chat is always in view).
10. **Game Tape and back:** the `Game Tape` link in the header chrome opens the shipped Game Tape view full-screen; `Back to the battle` returns to the board with your draft intact; on mobile the sheet is back at the detent it had.
11. **Remove the query string** once more: the three tabs, the Command Center tab with its Live Activity sub-tab, `DIRECTIVE LOCKED IN` and `Executing on next evaluation window` on the directive cards — all exactly as shipped (bug 2's PR retires those later).

19. **Branch.** `claude/battle-view-phase-a-layout-425nnm` = `…-v5gog5` rebased onto `def0fcbe` + A4. D-63 names `…-v5gog5`; the ledger row carries the note.
20. **The outage label is for a timeout only.** The cron persists `haikuError.failureClass ∈ {timeout, budget_skipped, truncated_response, an HTTP status, an error name, unknown}` (`api/_utils/agentEvalTransport.js` `classifyHaikuFailure`; `agent-evaluate.js` ~1957-2035) and the shipped Pulse never says "timed out" (it renders the engine's `Evaluation engine degraded this tick ({failureClass})`). So `… · the evaluation timed out` renders only when `failureClass === 'timeout'`; every other outage keeps the plain `No decision recorded at this check` (A4 review L1-F1, honesty rule 8). **Copy request:** a class-neutral line (e.g. `… · the evaluation did not complete`) if the founder wants every outage named.
21. **A guardrail-forced swap that then fails or is blocked** carries the system's `Guardrail override (…): …` text as its `rationale` (`agent-evaluate.js` ~2106-2121) and renders under `Argued for a swap …` / `The agent's own words …` — the agent did not argue for it (L1-F2; reachable, rare: a stop-loss-forced swap whose replacement has no valid price). **Copy request** + a branch on the persisted `guardrailOverrides` / `guardrailSourceNote`; recorded beside N5.
22. **The row's inner targets stay mouse-only (CC's call, F16).** The symbol (research modal) and points (breakdown) targets inside the row button are the shipped divs, out of the tab order; promoting them to real buttons is a flag-off markup change for the rows PR. The row button is named `Why? {symbol}` and **described** by its price change and proximity text (ids under the flag); the score header is named `Why? · the whole book` and described by the names / day / CPU blocks (review CR2 — without the description the scores would leave the accessibility tree).
23. **Game Tape is a full-screen overlay**, not a route: `position: fixed; inset: 0; z-index: 30` over the page, the header and the layout hidden beneath (`visibility`), the document scroll locked while it is up, the backdrop canvas unmounted, the chat left mounted (a draft survives), focus on the way back on open and on the link (or the opener) on close — never on mount (review CR6).
24. **The sheet's geometry.** Peek sizes itself to the handle row plus the composer (`height: auto`; the message list is collapsed at peek), so a draft never clips the send button — refuter A measured the shipped composer in Chromium under the production cascade (no Tailwind preflight; every button and textarea forced to 16 px by `index.css`) at 39 + 24 + 105 = 168 px with an EMPTY one-line draft, so a fixed 148 px budget clipped the send button by 28 px before anything was typed (review CR3 / L2-F6). `SHEET_PEEK_PX = 172` is now the floor for half / full and the clearance the mobile board reserves (204 px); half is 50 % and full is the viewport minus 56 px of the **visible** viewport (`visualViewport.height` where the browser has one, else `innerHeight`; L2-F11); a pull past 40 px on the grabber moves one detent. The same 16 px rule is why the handle's turn text sizes itself on its span.
25. **The sheet sits at the root**, a sibling of the layout container (review CR1 / L2-F1): a `position: fixed` sheet inside the layout's `z-index: 2` stacking context was painted over by the header at full.
26. **A breakpoint crossing (768 px) re-mounts `AgentChat`** (the column and the sheet are different parents): an unsent draft, an in-flight bubble and the error line are lost; exactly one `AgentChat` exists at every moment; `ensure-opener` does not re-fire (module-scoped `Set`) (L2-F4).
