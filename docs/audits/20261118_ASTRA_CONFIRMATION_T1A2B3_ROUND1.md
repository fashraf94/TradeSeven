## Five changes required for dark merge

### T2D-C01 · A revised ex-date conflict pays the buyer · T2D-01 / Q10, criterion 3

On the purchase date, the feed listed $1 and $2 for the same dividend. The next session it listed $1 and $3 for that same ex-date. A day later it re-dated the $3 row. The idea and comparison each received **$299.91**, and book cash rose from $80,000 to $80,299.91 at full quality. When $3 was among the original three payloads, the identical re-date paid $0. The old ID had a conflict receipt marked `entitled:false` and had never been applied or declined. The same path with an updated 7:4 split took the book from 99.970008 to 174.947514 shares at a flat $100. (Run:71, Fix:612, Fix:623, ledger.js:869–875, ledger.js:915–923.)

**Fix.** When an existing non-entitled conflict is observed again, retain each distinct newly observed economic payload for re-date matching. Pin the two successive payload sets and their re-date. This can be corrected before the first payment. (ledger.js:874, ledger.js:918–923.)

### T2D-C02 · An open archive/backfill disagreement can settle without the founder · T2D-02 / FQ-3, criterion 3

The archived 5:4 split and the backfilled 3:2 split disagreed under one ID. Catch-up held the position at **99.970008** shares as required. At the next live close, re-listing the 3:2 row under the same ID credited **149.955012** shares to the idea, comparison and book at $80, full quality. The founder’s subsequent choice of 5:4 returned **409**. With no re-listing, the record stayed open and the same request returned **200**. An archived session that repeated the backfill payload also settled the conflict during catch-up. The filter at the union protects only against the backfill row appearing alone. The receipt retains the competing ratios without recording that FQ-3 reserves settlement for the founder. (Run:72, close.js:246–254, dailyCoverage.js:100–113, ledger.js:869–875, ledger.js:947–963, Confirmation brief:103.)

A separate test delayed the first archived disagreement until after the cache row had already settled. The founder then received 404. That sequence falls under T2D-05’s existing settled-ID assignment. C02 above starts with a recorded, unsettled conflict. (Run:72, Build:703.)

**Fix.** Mark a same-ID archive/backfill disagreement on the ledger’s conflict receipt. While that receipt is open, require its founder ruling for settlement under the original or a moved date. Preserve ordinary feed-only conflict correction. (close.js:251–254, ledger.js:950–963, Fix:679–697.)

### T2D-C03 · One failed coverage day takes away the founder’s dividend choice · T2D-06 / FQ-2, criteria 3 and 5

Both runs saw the same $1/$2 conflict, then the old $1 row beside a new-date $2 row, then the new row alone, then the old row alone. I changed only the AAPL coverage flag on the day both dates appeared. With coverage, $199.94 was paid, the conflict remained open, and the founder’s $1 choice succeeded and took dividends to **$299.91**. With failed coverage, the new $2 row waited one day. Its replay retired the old ID as `settled_by_redate`, restored full quality and made the same request return **409**. Final dividends stayed at **$199.94**. The post-refuter test follows the old row alone immediately after the overlap. The intervening new-row-only session exposes the difference. (Run:73, Fix:733–751, ledger.js:932–940, ledger.js:946–964, Confirmation brief:102.)

**Fix.** Persist the fact that both interpretations were seen on the open conflict. A replayed or re-listed feed row can settle its own payment once. Only the founder can retire the ambiguous old record. (ledger.js:933, ledger.js:950–962, ledger.js:1064–1066.)

### T2D-C04 · A split simulated on an uncovered close erases the next halving · T2D-08, criteria 3 and 5

AAPL closed at $100, $50, $25 and $50. The $25 session had failed coverage, a $1.50 dividend, and a late 2:1 row explaining the **first** drop. The screening pass simulated that split and retired the original suspicion. The actual ledger held the split for coverage. Because the screening pass had retired the suspicion, the code skipped recording the move from $50 to $25. After recovery, each stock ledger received **$299.91** and the book showed full quality. Moving the same split’s first appearance to the recovery session retained the $25 record and kept the dividend at **$0**. The same pair gave $299.91 versus $0 when the dividend first appeared on recovery. A founder `price_move` on the first drop during the gap also reproduced the release. (Run:74, ledger.js:393–397, ledger.js:438–453, ledger.js:1000–1005, ledger.js:1203–1217, AmF:56.)

**Fix.** On an uncovered symbol, record a usable observed transition against the suspicion that still stands on the actual holding. A split that coverage prevents from applying cannot retire that suspicion in the transition decision. Keep the existing daily-reference and quote-quality checks. (close.js:310, ledger.js:440–452, Fix:657–673, Fix:702–712.)

### T2D-C05 · An idea-only conflict suppresses genuine comparison cash · T2D-02 / T2D-09 interaction, criterion 3

I used the reviewed frozen-basket setup, with one deliberate alteration to stored test data: only the idea retained the $1/$2 conflict receipt. The founder selected $2 for that ledger. A successful catch-up backfill carried a genuine $1.50 row for the same ID. The November 18 row credited the idea **$399.88** but wrote comparison cash **$0** and comparison value **$19,994.00**, at full quality. The comparison received **$149.96** on November 19. With the identical $1.50 row also listed in the November 18 archive, it received $149.96 on November 18 and its value was **$20,143.96**. Without a founder ruling, the backfill-only payment was still missing after the first 20-session pass. The close combines conflict IDs across ledgers and drops the backfill row before it prepares each ledger’s action map. (Run:75, Fix:388–414, close.js:251–254, close.js:299–323, close.js:511.)

**Fix.** Filter disputed backfill rows against each recipient ledger’s own open records. Preserve a genuine feed row on the comparison when only the idea’s conflict or ruling covers that ID. Assert the dated comparison cash and value on the ruling close. (close.js:251–254, close.js:302–308, holdRulings.js:255–274.)
