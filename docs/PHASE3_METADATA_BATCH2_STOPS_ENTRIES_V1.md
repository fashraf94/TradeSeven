## V1.3 findings (from the bridge verification)

8. **⚠ SELF-CAUGHT AUTHORING DEFECT — all three descriptors used invented token vocabulary.** V1.2 authored `trigger`/`side`/`resetBehavior`/`evaluationTiming` from plausible-sounding intent rather than the real contract; the verbatim quote shows **4 of 8 fields wrong on every descriptor**, and `matchSupportedShape` requires exact equality — so the "first exact-match binding" claim was false and all three would have failed to compile. Corrected above. **Standing rule for Batch 3+: a `guardrailBinding` may only be authored by copying the verbatim shape from `SUPPORTED_GUARDRAIL_SHAPES` and appending `valueParamKey`. Never author descriptor tokens from intent.** (Guide amendment candidate.)
9. **⚠ THE SHAPE CONTRACT ITSELF IS DEFECTIVE — `trailingStop.basis:'hwm'`.** The engine does not use a high-water mark; it reconstructs a modeled peak from ATR-multiplier history. A compiler matching on `basis:'hwm'` would compile a rule the engine enforces on a different line — approve-one-execute-another, at the contract layer. **Amendment-C-class item**, and the fix is a `compileBuild.js` correction (declare the real basis, e.g. `modeled_atr_peak`), not a rule change.
10. **No clamp on the bridge path.** Season-rule emitters clamp (3–20 stop, 3–25 trail, 15–50 sector) but `dimensionsToGuardrails` clamps nothing — an out-of-range dimension value reaches `deployedGuardrails` unclamped. Symmetric across all three, but it means the *same* dimension value is bounded differently by its two consumers. `paramBounds` input for cell authoring; possible defect in its own right.
11. **Bridge/engine honesty gaps confirmed at the bridge layer:** `maxPosition` is emitted `enforcement:'hard'` while the engine no-ops it (`skipped_incompatible`), and the engine reads **only** `type` and `value` — `unit` and `enforcement` are never consumed (`agentGuardrails.js:236-240`). The bridge's `enforcement:'hard'` label is decorative; enforcement is implied by type. Corroborates the census `maxPosition` finding and the Batch-1 vacuity ruling.
12. **`STRICTEST = min` for all three types** (`compileBuild.js:83`) — DR-4's strictest-wins is implemented as `min`, confirming the merge policy the spec assumed.
