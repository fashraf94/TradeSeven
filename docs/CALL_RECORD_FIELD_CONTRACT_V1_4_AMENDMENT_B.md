# Call Record Field Contract V1.4 — Amendment B (Build 1a semantics)

**Date:** 2026-10-02 (revision 2, after the Build 1a build) · **Author:** Fable · **Supersedes:** revision 1 of this file (committed-blob SHA-256 `fc610653e477371478e69a6ad68b3f277d19a737d9fcc15ce92073b1949466ea`, the pin in the Build 1a report §0) · **Amends:** `docs/CALL_RECORD_FIELD_CONTRACT_V1_4.md` (SHA `0b7d1d2a00a087902bc5fb3f73c5fad5ee8af3e275ab2abbebed77fff80b2667`). Where a clause below conflicts with V1.4, this amendment governs; everything else in V1.4 stands unchanged. **No field shape in §4 of V1.4 changes.** New fields are marked *additive*. Reader-derived values are marked *derived* and never stored.
**Why:** the founder ruled on Oct 2 that the answer loop is **coaching, not enforcement** (answers request; receipts record evidence), that `ask`, `keep`, the re-ask and second answers are deferred to Build 1b, and that four narrowings apply to Build 1a. Astra's two review rounds (`docs/review-cockpit-build1-spec-v1`, `-v1-1`) identified the exact V1.4 clauses these decisions touch. This amendment records them so no builder infers a contract change. Revision 2 folds the four readings the Build 1a build recorded for this chat (`docs/audits/<date>_BUILD1A_ANSWER_LOOP.md` §10.3): B.10's wording against what the sweep actually reads, the `until` reading in B.12, and two new clauses, B.13 (a pick traded for another option) and B.14 (`saidOk`).

| § | Clause (V1.4 reference) | Amended text |
|---|---|---|
| **B.1** | §7 answer legality and charging (V1.4:92-98) | **Build 1a answers:** `go`, `hold`, `go_now`, `pick`, `agree`, `disagree`. **Directive (charged one message, filed through the gate):** `hold` on an act-default call; `go_now` on a hold-default call; `pick` with a stored option on an open pick. **Acknowledgment (uncharged, no directive):** `go` on an act-default call; `hold` on a hold-default call; `agree`/`disagree` on a pick whose terminal outcome shows the agent's own choice — allowed whether the parent is active or completed. `disagree` is therefore an acknowledgment in 1a, not a divergent charged answer. |
| **B.2** | §4 answer enum (V1.4:63-65) | The enum is unchanged (`go, hold, ask, go_now, keep, pick, agree, disagree`). `ask` and `keep` are **deferred to Build 1b**; a 1a endpoint returns `400 deferred` for them and writes nothing. The re-ask and second answers are 1b. |
| **B.3** | §7 unheard-slot protection "at every writer" (V1.4:96) | Scoped to the **call family**: a pending call-family thread refuses a second **call-family** filing (`directive_pending`). Ordinary chat keeps HEAD's latest-wins without client belief; the chip keeps its belief check. An ordinary filing that replaces a call-family slot is recorded on the replacing exchange as `supersedes: { directiveThreadId, at }` (*additive*) and, at `on`, as a `superseded` call event; the call's `playerResponse` is never rewritten. |
| **B.4** | §7 directive lifetime "through the first applicable check" (V1.4:94) | A call directive has a **bounded advisory lifetime**: `expiresAtMs = horizon.expiresAt`, plus 900,000 ms for `next_check`. This is not an H2 guarantee: the call remains H2-eligible regardless, and a judgment after the lifetime may report "answer expired before this check" and never implies the answer was heard. Hearing does not consume a directive; `call_go`/`call_pick` retire on matched executor evidence or at lifetime end; `call_hold` at lifetime end. |
| **B.5** | §4 `refused.reason` (V1.4:67) | `refused` is **stored** only for `directive_pending` (shape unchanged). Other refusals (`already_answered`, `expired`, `belief_mismatch`, `budget`, `parent_not_active`) are returned to the caller and not persisted. |
| **B.6** | §4 "agent-initiated receipt exchange" (V1.4 §4, §7) | Receipts, acknowledgments and every player-facing line about a call live in **`agentBattles/{battleId}/callEvents/{eventId}`** (*additive* subcollection, owner-read, server-write), merged into chat at render. They never enter `chatExchanges[]`. Deterministic ids: `${evalId}:declared`, `${callId}:answered:${answerId}`, `${callId}:heard:${evalId}`, `${callId}:acted:${evalId}`, `${callId}:no_match:${evalId}`, `${callId}:expired`, `${callId}:ended`, `${directiveThreadId}:superseded`; `answerId = ${callId}:answer:${answer}:${pickSymbol ?? ''}`. An event is created inside the transaction that commits its transition. |
| **B.7** | §6 completion / `ended_with_battle` (V1.4 §6, §9) | **Completion writes nothing to calls.** `completeBattle` is untouched. Open calls under a terminal parent are closed by the Build 1a sweep (`ended_with_battle`, hit outcomes preserved, sweep receipt with `px: null`, `evalId: null`, `source: 'sweep'`, `reason: 'battle_ended'`). The sweep's unobserved-expiry policy for `next_check` (the one H2 reserved) is: expire only when the regular ET session containing the slot has closed (`maintenanceNowMs >= session.closeMs`) and the call is still open under a fresh active parent; receipt `reason: 'unobserved'`. |
| **B.8** | §7 chat calls block classes (V1.4:102) | In 1a the block renders **open calls** and **the five newest resolved calls**; the **"hit awaiting an answer"** class is empty until 1b (reserved, not removed). |
| **B.9** | §4/§7 receipt vocabulary (V1.4 §4 `outcome`, §7) | 1a receipts state three facts only: **heard** (the committed entry's unsuppressed `heard.directiveThreadId` equals the answer's thread; time = that check's `promptBuiltAt`), **acted** (`outcome.actedEvalId` via the whole-trade matcher, extended with the selected pick), and **no matching trade at this check** (the check heard the thread and its present, parsed executor result does not match the call's leg). **`outcome.heldOff` is not set in 1a** ("held" is not provable without a complete action set); the field stays in the shape, unused. `playerResponse.heardEvalId` is first-confirmed and monotonic. Receipts cover a call through its resolution; post-resolution action reconciliation is 1b. |
| **B.10** | §9 off composition (V1.4:109-118) | One explicit exception to "no reads at off": when the global mode is `on` and a battle's owner is not allowlisted (per-owner `off`), the sweep **may read that battle's `callSweepQueue` row, the `callSweepState` cursor and the battle's own parent document** (the owner and status live only there), **may page `agentBattles` by `expiresAt` in its reconciliation phase, and writes the `callSweepState` cursor once per invocation**; it reads no call, declaration, receipt or event for that battle and writes nothing to it. The sweep runs only at global `on`: at global `off` and `shadow` it returns before any read, and the cron and endpoint perform no calls I/O. Call-family directives are inactive at every reader unless the cron has attached the resolved mode and check instant in memory. History windows filter cockpit filings before slicing when the resolved mode is not `on`. |
| **B.11** | §4 top-level `directiveThreadId` (V1.4 §4) | Set at answer acceptance to the filed directive's `directiveThreadId` (same value as `playerResponse.directiveThreadId`). The `declared` event's `promptDirectiveThreadId` (*additive*, event-level) records a directive present in the declaring check's prompt — **prompt inclusion, not causation**; causal attribution of a fork to a directive requires an explicit per-fork field (round 3) behind `RESPONSE_FORK_ATTRIBUTION_ENABLED`. |
| **B.12** | §7 canonical text (V1.4:92-94) | Canonical templates `callActions.v1`, rendered from the stored call only (no player text): **hold** — `Hold off on the ${symbol} ${direction === 'exit' ? 'exit' : 'entry'} until ${untilText}.`, where `untilText` is the deadline phrase with its leading preposition removed (`by today's close` → `today's close`, `by the next check` → `the next check`, `before the battle ends` → `the battle ends`, `by 3:30 PM` → `3:30 PM`) · **go_now** — `If ${symbol} ${side} ${fmtPrice(level)} ${deadlineText}, go ahead and ${direction === 'exit' ? `exit${counterpart ? ` for ${counterpart}` : ''}` : `bring it in${counterpart ? ` for ${counterpart}` : ''}`}.` · **pick** — `Bring in ${pickSymbol} for ${swapOut} at the next check.` `deadlineText` as in Build 1a Spec V1.2 §4. The same text is shown to the player and to the model. |
| **B.13** | §7 receipts for a pick (new; B.9 extended) | For a **pick**, the request is the selection. A check that heard the `call_pick` directive and whose present, parsed executor result is anything other than the selected swap (`pickSymbol` in, `swapOut` out, the call's slot) records **`no_matching_trade`**, and the receipt names both the committed trade and the selection it was not ("The agent exited KO for AMD at the 10:30 check — not the selected JPM"). When the swap-out has left the book, the `call_pick` directive is retired (compare-and-clear). A result in another slot keeps the generic receipt and the directive. |
| **B.14** | §10 event shape, `saidOk` (new) | On the `declared` event, `saidOk` is the aggregate lint verdict over the declared calls' own `said`: `true` when every one passes, `false` when any fails, `null` when none was said. It is a summary only; whether a given call's sentence is shown is decided per call at read time by the lint. |

**Precedence and pin.** This amendment supersedes the referenced V1.4 clauses for Build 1a and later. The pilot handoff cites V1.4's SHA and this file's committed-blob SHA together. `docs/design/COCKPIT_SPEC_V1_3_AMENDMENT_A.md` is unaffected.
