## ⚠ C-21 — THE ACTION-PRECEDENCE CONTRACT (stated before the cells, per R1-F2)
V1.0 applied a carve-out test inconsistently — mb-08's *absence* of an exception produced a core_conflict while mb-10's *narrow* exception produced neutrals — and never stated the contract that test depends on. **The contract has a factual answer in this codebase, not a choice:**

What a rule-level swap-block *can* do is bind the **model's discretionary behavior** — its profit-side exits, opportunity-cost rotations, and thesis-driven swaps.

**Consequences applied throughout:** (a) **R-125 falls to tension** — mb-08 cannot override the Guardian's protective exits because nothing advisory can, so the identity's core mechanism survives and what remains is real but bounded friction on discretionary exits. (c) Receipts may claim an advisory instruction was *rendered*, never that a guardrail value changed; acceptance tests verify prompt rendering and behavioral interpretation, not guardrail mutation.
