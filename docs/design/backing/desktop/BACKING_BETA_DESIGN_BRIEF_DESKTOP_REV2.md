# Claude Design brief — Backing desktop, revision 2

**Status:** the desktop pass is approved as drawn for the Backing screen, Your Backing, results, and the private record — keep all of it. This round adds what's missing and brings it in line with the founder's September 23 rulings (Amendment C). Hard constraints from the original brief are unchanged and absolute.

---

## 1. The desktop League landing — missing, and the first priority

The desktop brief's §1 wasn't drawn: there is no desktop League landing, so the strip still has no desktop home. Design it on the **real desktop League**: three columns — *Live now · Follows* left, *Pick a draft slot* (the ranked-entry times) centre, *Leaderboard · The Field* right.

- The strip sits in the **centre column, directly under the draft slots**. All four week-states.
- A second version for a player **already seated this week** (the centre shows their game where the slot picker was): the strip sits directly under that, never pushed below "watch a live game" or the bracket line.
- No bracket running (today's production): no reserved space for the funnel. Bracket running: the funnel takes its place, nothing else moves.
- Pod rows' affordance reads **"Predictions"**.
- **"Watch a live game · spectate a live pod"** — propose whether it stays plain watching or also points to backing. One proposal, we'll rule.
- Show the desktop landing beside the mobile landing so placement can be compared.

## 2. Team labels are the agent's name (new ruling)

Wherever a team gets **one label** — pod rows, the winner line, Your Backing cards, the strip, the stake confirmation — the label is **the primary agent's name** (e.g. "Shadow"). The player's name appears as secondary text where there's room. The team card keeps its human-and-agent unit exactly as drawn. Update the mock data and every single-label spot accordingly.

## 3. Topping up a stake (new ruling)

A backer now holds **one stake per team**; backing the same team again **adds to it**. Design the stake control's top-up state: the existing stake shown, the amount being added, the new total against the 500 cap, and the confirm line **"Adds to your 250 BP on Shadow."**

Because "top-up" now means this, change the allowance line that currently reads *"no carry, no top-up"* to *"no carry-over, no refills"* so the two don't collide.

## 4. The 18+ / terms step — missing on desktop

The stake flow's first-time attestation (confirm 18 or older, accept the beta terms) isn't drawn for desktop. Place it where the stake control lives, shown once before a player's first stake. Copy stays as placeholder text marked for counsel.

## 5. Trainer stats — no desktop home

The player's private trainer stats ("beta stats": how many people backed your team, how much, how they did) exist on mobile under the pitch in the profile/agent area. Give them a desktop home in the same place. Private, never a ranking.

## 6. Pick one stake placement

The design leaves the stake control's position as a toggle (right column vs centre). **Choose one** — right column is the lean, provided the three disclosure lines stay visible above Confirm without scrolling at 1440×900.

## 7. Unchanged and still absolute

No pot, per-team amount or share, payout multiple, "most backed", or activity signal while a pool is open; backer count capped at three; signals frozen once qualified; loadout counts only; the lexicon (never bet, wager, odds, cash out, gamble); no leaderboard of backers; no currency purchase; no in-week staking.
