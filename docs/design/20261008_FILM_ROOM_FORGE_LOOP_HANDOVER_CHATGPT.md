## Purpose and requested decision

Please compare this direction with the Film Room expansion work already
underway in our Claude conversation. I want Film Room to lead useful
reflection with the agent, produce durable outputs, and contribute to
the development of both the profile partner and its trading archetypes.
I also want Forge to feel simpler and have a clear role in applying
supported adjustments.

The central proposal is one connected process: Film Room examines
experience, the learning process qualifies possible lessons, Forge
manages applicable configuration, and later games produce evidence for
the next review. Command Center makes retained changes and subsequent
use visible.

I agreed with this broad direction and asked to explore it further. The
detailed layouts, artifact structure, approval process, and sequence
below remain proposals for reconciliation. This handover requests an
opinion and a merged plan. It does not authorize implementation, replace
an existing specification, or establish current production readiness.

## Responsibilities across the product

| **Area**       | **Primary responsibility**                                                                                                                  |
|----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Command Center | Maintain the ongoing partnership, shared research, recent activity, and one useful next action. Show supported development and its sources. |
| Cockpit        | Explain live game decisions and invite useful input through the game’s permitted controls.                                                  |
| Film Room      | Review decisions and outcomes, examine uncertainty, resolve prior questions, and prepare meaningful next steps.                              |
| Forge          | Explain and manage the archetype’s active configuration, eligible recommendations, and trials for future deployments.                       |

## What success should feel like

After a review, the user understands what the agent retained, whether
anything changes, and where the next result will appear. Individuality
develops through research interests, explicit preferences, supported
customizations, and evaluated game experience. Film Room contributes
substantially to this development. Forge keeps its applicable effects
understandable.

# The proposed Film Room experience

The agent should prepare the review and bring the user a small number of
meaningful choices. The user contributes judgment, context, and consent.
They should not need to diagnose the whole game, write a lesson from
scratch, or invent the next assignment.

**1 Resolve earlier work.** Start with an open question, prior review
commitment, or trial touched by the latest game. Explain what new
evidence changes and what remains unresolved. If nothing relevant
occurred, say so.

**2 Recommend one case.** Prepare one useful decision for review and
explain why it deserves attention. Additional cases and deeper
inspection remain optional. Case selection needs an explicit,
evidence-based rule and reconciliation with Build A’s restrictions on
unsupported rankings.

**3 Reconstruct the decision.** Show what the agent received at decision
time, the recorded rationale, the equipped archetype and relevant
controls, the action or hold, and the observed outcome. Label recorded
facts, later reconstruction, and hypothetical comparisons separately.

**4 Examine the interpretation.** Explain what went well, what went
poorly, and which alternative explanations remain plausible. A losing
trade with sound reasoning and a winning trade with weak reasoning are
both valid findings. Positive restraint and useful research deserve
attention alongside mistakes.

**5 Offer a meaningful choice.** Depending on the evidence, offer to
examine another explanation, save an explicit partnership preference,
track an open question, or start an eligible trial. The agent explains
its recommendation and accepts disagreement. Choosing no change is a
valid outcome.

**6 Record the result.** Finish with a precise receipt stating what
persisted, its owner, its source, any behavioral effect, its effective
timing, and the next observable step. A failed save must not appear
complete.

## Depth and participation

Preserve the earlier brief review, study, and deep inspection structure.
A short review should stand on its own. Keep participation optional and
retain the earlier decision against Film Room XP, streaks, or rewards
for agreement. Game evidence should accrue even when the user never
opens the review.

## Why users return

The attraction is visible follow-through. The agent remembers a prior
discussion, finds relevant evidence, explains a disagreement, and
returns with an answer. A later game shows a traceable use of an
approved change. Growth displays summarize these records. Animation,
review counts, and flattering summaries do not establish learning.

## Grounding before narration

Agent-led presentation depends on a working review grounding path.
Narration describes the evidence and proposes interpretations.
Computation and the qualification process determine outcomes and lesson
maturity. A missing quote, unreliable result, or absent decision-time
record should remain an explicit gap or route to platform repair.

# Artifacts and their consequences

Each output needs a clear destination and a clear consequence. The user
should understand the effect of saving or approving it without
classifying technical record types themselves.

| **Output**                       | **Destination and effect**                                                                                                                               |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Reviewed decision                | Retain the case, evidence, interpretation, and uncertainty. It explains an episode without automatically changing behavior.                              |
| Confirmed partnership preference | Retain in the profile partner’s memory. Example: compare both stocks using the same criteria when explaining a replacement. Record its later use.        |
| Open question                    | Track relevant future evidence for a defined question. Scope trading questions to the appropriate archetype and game. Contrary evidence remains visible. |
| User requested adjustment        | Preview a supported customization and its scope. Route approval through the existing equip controls. Label it as the user’s choice.                      |
| Lesson candidate and trial       | Preserve the claim, evidence, maturity, and supported control target. A qualified candidate starts a scoped trial after consent.                         |
| Follow through record            | Connect a later decision to the applicable artifact and record its actual use and outcome. This supports the next review and Command Center display.     |

## Two forms of development

Shadow or Cipher, used here as the user-facing profile partner identity,
retains confirmed preferences, shared research, and corrections to its
understanding of the user. This naming does not imply a completed
migration or two separate required agents.

The user’s development record for a particular archetype holds
applicable behavioral candidates and trials. Switching archetypes
preserves the relationship and shared questions. It does not
automatically transfer an untested behavioral adjustment. Personal
development also does not rewrite the canonical archetype for every
user.

## Evidence requirements remain intact

Preserve Hunch, Testable, and Trial-proven maturity meanings. A Hunch
remains informational and unequippable as a learned lesson. Approval
permits a qualified trial. Approval itself does not prove the claim.
User-requested customization remains distinct from evidence-qualified
learning and must not relabel a weak lesson to bypass qualification.

Evaluate behavior across eligible episodes with denominators and
contrary evidence. Keep discovery evidence separate from later
confirmation. Preserve the existing limits on one-step counterfactuals
and per-user market-alpha claims. A profitable episode or favorable
replay does not establish durable advantage.

Protective exits need their own treatment, including protection value
and adverse outcomes. Ordinary swap regret must not teach the agent to
remove its safeguards. BaggerBomb evidence also does not establish
applicability to League or another ruleset.

# The proposed Forge organization

Forge should explain how the archetype is configured, why each
meaningful adjustment exists, and what is proposed for a future
deployment. Users should understand their setup before opening an
editor.

## What is active

Lead with the selected archetype and a short description of its
behavior. Show the meaningful customizations affecting it. Each item
explains what changes, where it came from, its applicability, and its
effective timing. Link back to the originating conversation, review, or
evaluated lesson.

Distinguish the configuration running in an active game from changes
prepared for a future deployment. Preserve existing game snapshots. An
item with incomplete historical provenance should state what is unknown.

## Recommended next

Show one or two useful proposals rather than a large unfiltered queue. A
proposal explains the supported adjustment, evidence or explicit user
intent, tradeoff, applicable archetype, and next action. If adoption
requires replacing an existing adjustment, present the replacement
before confirmation. Never silently remove an equipped item.

The action depends on its meaning. An explicit user customization
changes a supported setting through the normal controls. A sufficiently
qualified lesson starts a trial. An unresolved hypothesis remains a
question for further observation.

## What we are testing

Show the active trial, qualifying observations, remaining uncertainty,
and the next evaluation step. Provide straightforward stop and rollback
actions. Keep evidence maturity, trial progress, and current equip state
distinguishable. A trial without relevant new episodes should not imply
progress.

## Advanced work and history

Retain access to detailed editors, watchlists, rule bundles, character
controls, and historical evidence as appropriate. The proposal
simplifies the main experience. It does not require deleting reusable
controls or creating another learning store. Reconcile these views with
the earlier three-bench design before choosing navigation.

## One approval across Film Room and Forge

A user should approve an eligible adjustment from its Film Room card
without manually moving it into Forge. Both sections display the same
proposal and authoritative state. The same validated equip process
applies regardless of where approval occurs. Opening Forge later should
reveal the resulting configuration and its source without a second
approval.

Forge’s proposal or history record has no independent trading authority.
Only the existing supported configuration and execution controls
determine behavior. Compatibility, capacity, archetype identity, and
game eligibility still apply.

## Keep unrelated outputs in their proper homes

Partnership preferences belong with the profile partner. Unresolved
questions belong in the development record. Data defects belong with
platform repairs. Forge surfaces these when they explain a relevant
setting or trial, rather than asking the user to sort every review
artifact.

# Shared contract and an example journey

The first planning deliverable is a short contract shared by Command
Center, Film Room, and Forge. It should establish responsibilities
before detailed interface or database choices become fixed. Reuse
existing records and services wherever their semantics fit.

**Identity and scope.** Identify the user, stable partner, relevant
archetype and version, game or ruleset, and source review or decision. A
renamed partner or changed screen selection must not redirect historical
evidence.

**Meaning and authority.** Distinguish a retained interpretation, user
preference, open question, user customization, and qualified lesson.
State whether confirmation saves information, prepares an adjustment, or
authorizes an eligible trial.

**Source and lifecycle.** Preserve the originating evidence and
versions. Define pending, confirmed, dismissed, withdrawn, superseded,
expired, and trial states only where applicable. Keep consent, maturity,
and equip status distinct.

**Application and timing.** Describe the supported target, compatibility
checks, any replacement, and when the change takes effect. Read the
authoritative equipped configuration rather than maintaining a competing
status in each screen.

**Use and failure.** Record later retrieval or application before
displaying “used.” Define behavior for retries, failed persistence,
stale proposals, changed evidence, and withdrawal. Avoid duplicate
approvals or duplicate application.

## Hypothetical discretionary swap review

The agent sold a stock which later recovered, and the replacement
underperformed. Film Room presents the recorded reason, relevant
decision-time evidence, and a supported one-step comparison. It treats
the result as an episode worth examining.

The agent proposes tracking whether similar discretionary swaps
repeatedly leave near-threshold positions without enough replacement
benefit. The user agrees to track the question. No trading adjustment
follows from this approval. Command Center shows the open investigation
and its source.

Later, sufficient evidence qualifies an existing archetype-compatible
adjustment for a trial. The agent explains the tradeoff. The user
approves once from Film Room. Forge shows the approved trial and its
future deployment scope.

A later eligible game uses the approved configuration. The decision
record identifies whether the adjustment applied and how it influenced
the action. The cockpit exposes this connection where supported. Film
Room examines the subsequent evidence, including unfavorable cases.
Forge reflects whether the trial continues, ends, or produces a retained
supported adjustment.

## What this example proves and leaves open

The intended proof is traceable continuity from review to consent,
configuration, later use, and renewed evaluation. Outcome attribution
needs its own reconciled method. Total points earned after equip do not
establish points caused by the adjustment. The concrete adjustment,
thresholds, and implementation details await the existing arc’s review.

## First proof journey

Start with one prepared review case, one meaningful user choice, one
retained preference or open question, one later relevant use, and one
return review. A confirmed partnership preference offers an early proof
of continuity without claiming improved trading. An open question offers
a useful path when the evidence is insufficient for a change.

Forge’s first contribution is an accurate explanation of the current
configuration and known sources. The first release should distinguish
retained information from applicable trading adjustments. Learned
behavioral trials follow their existing qualification requirements.

## Acceptance checks

The artifact survives reload. Its source and owner remain visible. A
later independent interaction records its actual use. Withdrawal affects
future use as specified. Archetype switching preserves relationship
history without transferring unqualified behavior. Active games retain
their deployment configuration. Missing evidence produces an honest
unresolved state. Film Room and Forge agree on the same proposal and
application status.

## Work scheduling

Visual design, presentation components, fixture-based prototypes, and
isolated read-only views fit alongside Command Center after their
interfaces stabilize. Shared memory, equip behavior, trading prompts,
and deployment snapshots require coordinated implementation and review.
The recommendation is one behavior-changing build under review at a
time.

Claude Design remains the lead for Command Center visuals. Existing tape
recovery, result integrity, and review grounding work continue within
their current ownership. Their readiness governs the claims exposed by
the review experience. This handover sets no new deadline or production
activation date.
