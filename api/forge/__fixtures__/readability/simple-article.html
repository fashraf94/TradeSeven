Apple Beats Q4 Earnings, Guides Higher On Services

By Staff Reporter — November 2025

Apple Inc. (AAPL) reported fiscal fourth-quarter earnings that topped Wall Street expectations on Thursday, with revenue of $94.9 billion versus a consensus estimate of $94.4 billion. Services revenue, including App Store, Apple Music, and iCloud subscriptions, hit a record $24.2 billion, growing 14% year over year and continuing to outpace hardware growth.

iPhone revenue came in at $46.2 billion, slightly below the $46.6 billion analysts modeled, but management emphasized that pre-orders for the iPhone 17 Pro lineup are running ahead of last year's cycle. CFO Luca Maestri said gross margins expanded to 46.8%, the highest in the company's history, helped by a richer Services mix and improved component pricing.

For the December quarter, Apple guided revenue growth in the mid-single digits with Services accelerating into the mid-teens. Shares rose about 3% in after-hours trading on the report.
